Important: your numbers live in this browser only. If you clear your browser data, they are gone — unless you exported a backup. Export after every big change and keep the file somewhere safe.

How this works

Safe-to-Spend = paycheck income − bills due before next payday − savings set aside − money already spent this cycle.

Today shows one next step only: the nearest unpaid bill due within 2 days, then any waitlist item whose 48 hours are up, then logging today's spending. If nothing needs you, it says so.

Your streak counts the days you opened the app. It only ever grows — missing a day never breaks it and nothing here turns red.

The waitlist holds a want for 48 hours. After that you decide: buy it, or skip it and keep the money.

Debt runs Snowball (smallest balance first) or Avalanche (highest interest first) and works out your debt-free date.
